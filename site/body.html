      Fleet coverage and the two loose ends

      100 boats are scored in the championship. The event's analysis covers 34 of them, and 14 have telemetry here — so every fleet-relative figure above is a 14-boat sample, not a fleet result.

      The pipeline behind this page

      Everything lives on one branch and deploys itself. Wind refreshes on GitHub's servers, so nothing needs to be open locally.

      How this was produced, and what is not in it
      • Telemetry — 20 Vakaros Atlas .vkx logs decoded from the Drive folder. Position, SOG and COG at 0.5 s; positions are int32 degrees × 1e-7, COG in radians, plus a unit orientation quaternion on every fix.
      • Wind — today from DWD ICON-EU 06z (7 km) and ECMWF IFS 06z (0.25°); tomorrow from a live three-model consensus (ICON-EU, ECMWF IFS, GFS) at the race area.
      • The correction — the mean of the committee line square and the fleet's first-beat bearing, compared with the forecast at the midpoint of each beat. Both inputs are independent of the model.
      • No wind instrument — no boat logged one, so every observed bearing is inferred from GPS geometry and no measured wind speed exists.
      • Not derived from telemetry — finishing positions, elapsed times, points and penalties. Mark positions are inferred from track reversals, not recorded.
      • Manoeuvre counts — from speed dips. Upwind tack counts are reliable; downwind gybe counts over-count, because surfing makes speed oscillate.
      • Heel and pitch — derivable from the quaternion, but not yet computed.
